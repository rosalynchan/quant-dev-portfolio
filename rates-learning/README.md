# Rates Desk Engineering Learning Series

A practical, desk-oriented learning series that connects core Rates concepts with front-office engineering.

The series is written from the perspective of a Python developer building tools around pricing, market data, risk, PnL, controls, and trader workflows. Each article aims to develop both financial intuition and the ability to translate desk requirements into reliable software.

## What each article includes

- A realistic Rates desk scenario
- Clear explanations of the relevant financial concepts
- A worked numerical example
- Engineering implications and Python-style pseudocode
- Data quality, validation, and production considerations
- Requirement-discovery and interview questions
- Practical takeaways for front-office developers

## Article index

New articles are proposed as dated Markdown files in this directory and reviewed through pull requests before publication.

## Public-safety policy

All examples are generic and educational. This series does not contain confidential employer information, proprietary models or architecture, client information, internal system names, or investment advice.
